    Roadmap to the platinum — about 200 hours

    PowerPyx rates the platinum 4/10: long, not hard. One playthrough is enough, there are no difficulty settings, no mission medals, no online trophies — and free-roam stays open after the story. Work the three steps in order; every trophy in this guide is tagged with the step it belongs to.

    Before you start

    • No cheats. Turning on a cheat disables trophies and saving (PowerPyx).
    • Keep manual saves. There are 15 manual save slots and no mission replay — save at the start and end of every chapter, and rotate the rest for free-roam cleanup (PowerPyx).
    • Do the Chapter 1 meditation. It is the one thing that might not come back — see Missables.
    • Scan and study as you go. WAiNK-scan every new vehicle and study every new animal through binoculars; both feed the 100% checklist and two trophies.

    Missables — only one trophy to watch

    PowerPyx flags a single trophy as potentially missable. Everything else is safe: all trophy-related quest givers come back after the story, free-roam stays open, and there is no mission replay to worry about. Keep a manual save at the start of each chapter anyway — with 15 save slots, it costs nothing.

    Why the rest are safe

    • Optional missions that disappear (black icons with pink letters) do not matter for trophies — there are far more than the 100 the checklist wants (PowerPyx).
    • Quest givers who vanish for a while — Kenton, for example — all return later in the story (PowerPyx).
    • Strangers & Freaks strands, all 8 of them, stay in free-roam after the credits (PowerPyx).
    • Nothing on the 100% checklist is missable; it is all doable after the story (PowerPyx).
